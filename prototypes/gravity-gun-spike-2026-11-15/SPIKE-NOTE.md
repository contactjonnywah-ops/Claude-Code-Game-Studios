# Spike note: gravity gun

PROTOTYPE - NOT FOR PRODUCTION

- **Question tested:** Does shooting a surface to make it the ground feel good for the player
  and physics objects?
- **Result:** YES, it works. In the developer's words after play (2026-11-15): "Physics and
  mechanics are sound for a prototype."
- **What was built:** one first-person test chamber in the browser. The shot surface becomes
  the ground for the player and six crates; a landing above 19 m/s kills; the crosshair warns
  of a deadly fall.
- **Not tested:** ramps or any surface that is not axis-aligned, tumbling crates, a puzzle
  built on the mechanic, shooting crates.
- **What to do next:** the developer's call. The mechanic is worth building on: the next step
  is a game brief for the full puzzle platformer (`/prototype` with the idea, or `/brainstorm`),
  with this spike as its reference. The spike's code is not carried into production.
