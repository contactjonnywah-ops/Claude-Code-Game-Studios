# Game Brief: Ember Moon

## In your words
Ember Moon (picked from brainstorm: "shaman on a tiny spinnable moon, command followers by planting totems, crack the crust to swallow rival camps"). Asked to study Bullfrog's Populous 3 and brainstorm a spiritual remake.
1–8. you pick

**Pitch:** Spin a tiny burning moon, plant totems to steer your tribe, and crack the crust open to swallow the rival camp whole. *(provisional)*

**Genre:** god game / small RTS *(inferred (strong))*

**Controls:** drag to spin the moon, wheel to zoom; click the shaman, right-click to walk her; 1/2/3 plant a Gather / Build / War totem under the cursor; Q/W/E pick Fissure / Raise / Ember, click to cast. *(guessed)*

**Question:** is reshaping the ground under the rival camp more fun than fighting it? *(guessed)*

**Hooks:** the battlefield is the weapon — a fissure swallows huts and followers, raised land makes bridges and walls; followers obey totems, not micro. *(provisional)*

**Intent:** payoff builds *(provisional)*

## Look and references
- Look: small low-poly moon of dark basalt; glowing ember cracks pulse along fault lines; dusk violet sky with a big gas-giant on the horizon. Your followers warm orange robed figures with a staff, rivals cold blue. Totems are carved poles topped with a glowing glyph. Signature detail: an opened fissure glows molten and slowly cools shut. Every figure drawn, no emoji, no plain circles. *(provisional)*
- References: Populous: The Beginning (weak respawning shaman, mana from followers, terrain spells, rotatable world), mechanics only. *(you said)*
- View: 3D · Camera: orbit around the globe, drag-rotate, zoom. *(provisional)*

## First minute
Moon spins into view; 6 followers by a hearth, shaman beside them. A glowing hint: "Press 1 on the trees". You plant a Gather totem; followers walk off and haul wood. Press 2: a Build totem; a hut rises from the wood. Mana bar fills as followers work. Spin the moon: the rival camp appears over the horizon. Walk the shaman to the land's edge, W: Raise lifts a land bridge toward them *(guessed)*.

## Challenge
First rival raid around minute 3: 3 blue warriors over the land bridge or the shallows, warned 15 s ahead with a red ping and an alert. Each raid bigger. Fissure costs a lot of mana and has short range, so the shaman must walk into danger to cast it; if she dies she respawns at the hearth after 10 s. Losing every follower loses; a loss restarts the moon *(provisional)*.

## Core loop
- Plant totems: followers gather wood and build huts; huts add followers and mana.
- Spend mana: Raise bridges/walls, Ember raiders.
- Walk the shaman within range of the rival camp and Fissure it: huts and followers fall in.
- Rival raids back harder; rebuild, push again.

## Goal & fail state
- Win: swallow or burn every rival hut. *(provisional)*
- Lose: every follower dead (shaman alone cannot save the tribe). *(provisional)*

## Features
- [ ] spinnable low-poly moon with land, sea and deformable height
- [ ] totems: Gather / Build / War; followers obey the nearest
- [ ] wood + mana economy: huts spawn followers, working followers make mana
- [ ] shaman: weak, respawns at the hearth, only caster
- [ ] 3 spells with range and mana cost: Fissure, Raise, Ember
- [ ] rival tribe camp sending bigger timed raids with a warning
- [ ] win / lose screens and restart
